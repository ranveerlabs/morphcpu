journal entry template
======================

kept as .txt on purpose. the submission platform scans every .md in the repo and
splits it by heading, so a template full of empty headings gets parsed as a pile
of real entries and every one of them fails validation. .txt is invisible to it.

same reason the entries themselves use **bold labels** instead of ### headings.
every ### becomes its own entry needing 100+ chars and an image, which no
subsection is ever going to satisfy.

three things each entry has to have or it gets flagged:
  - Time spent: on its own line, capitalised, nothing after it on that line.
    "19m" or "1h 47m" or "3:15". thats what the parser matches on
  - 100+ characters of content, links dont count toward it
  - at least one image

be specific. this thing is the evidence that real work happened:
  - exact part numbers. ICE40UP5K-SG48I not "the fpga"
  - the datasheet section you actually read, with the page or table number
  - why you picked something AND what you rejected. a decision with no
    alternative considered isnt a decision its a default
  - real numbers. clock freqs, utilisation, current draw, trace widths, timing
    slack, baud rates, resistor values and the maths behind them
  - failures in as much detail as successes. what broke, the exact error text,
    what the cause actually turned out to be

write it like you talk. no symptom/cause/fix label scaffolding, just say what
happened and what fixed it.
